# Developer feedback on 1C

The [source register](reviews.csv) summarizes nine Hacker News comments from 2011 to 2023. Several commenters discuss readability and the scripting language. Others describe experience with the accounting software or work opportunities for developers. The sample comes from one developer forum and contains no star ratings.

One commenter described difficulties using tools outside the Windows accounting ecosystem for Russian tax filings in 2011. That observation records the commenter's experience at the time. [Original discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=2990150)

The sample gives limited evidence about the priorities of US accountants. Interviews with potential 4VEKO users would help test those priorities.
